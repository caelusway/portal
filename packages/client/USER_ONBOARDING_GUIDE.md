# Portal User Onboarding Guide

## 🧬 Welcome to Portal: Your BioDAO Creation Journey

Portal is designed to help scientists and researchers launch their own Decentralized Science (DeSci) communities called BioDAOs. This guide will walk you through every step of the process.

## 📱 **Getting Started: Creating Your Account**

### Step 1: Visit Portal

Navigate to the Portal platform in your web browser. No downloads or special software required!

Portal uses Privy for secure, gasless authentication. Here's what you'll see:

1. Click **"Get Started"** or **"Login"**
2. Select **"Continue with Email"**
3. Enter your email address (any email works - university, Gmail, etc.)
4. Check your email for a verification link

✅ Portal automatically creates a secure blockchain wallet for you  
✅ All transactions are gasless (free for you)  
✅ No need to understand crypto or manage private keys  
✅ Your wallet works across all devices

## 🎯 **Understanding Your BioDAO Journey: The 4-Level System**

Portal guides you through a structured progression to build a credible scientific community:

### 🌟 **Level 1: Science NFT Creation**

**Goal**: Establish your scientific identity on the blockchain

**What you'll do**:

1. **Mint Idea NFT**: Represents your core research concept
2. **Mint Vision NFT**: Represents your long-term scientific goals

**How it works**:

- CoreAgent (your AI guide) will walk you through the process
- Use the NFT minting interface in the Portal dashboard
- All minting is gasless - no fees for you
- Each NFT gets unique AI-generated artwork

**[Screenshot placeholder: NFT minting interface]**

**Why this matters**: These NFTs serve as timestamped proof of your scientific ideas and establish credibility in the DeSci ecosystem.

### 🗣️ **Level 2: Discord Community Setup**

**Goal**: Create your research community hub

**Requirements**:

- Create a Discord server for your project
- Install Portal's verification bot
- Grow to **4+ members**

**Step-by-step process**:

#### A. Create Your Discord Server

1. Open Discord (app or web browser)
2. Click the **"+"** button to create a new server
3. Choose **"Create My Own"**
4. Name it after your research project
5. Create relevant channels (e.g., #general, #research, #papers)

#### B. Get the Bot Installation Link

1. In Portal, chat with CoreAgent
2. Ask: _"I've created my Discord server, how do I add the bot?"_
3. CoreAgent will provide a custom bot installation link
4. Click the link and authorize the bot for your server

#### C. Share Your Invite Link

1. In Discord, right-click your server name
2. Select **"Invite People"**
3. Click **"Edit invite link"**
4. Set it to **"Never expire"**
5. Copy the link and paste it in your Portal chat with CoreAgent

**[Screenshot placeholder: Discord server setup and bot installation]**

#### D. Grow Your Community

**Strategies for reaching 4+ members**:

- Invite research colleagues
- Share in academic social media
- Post in relevant research forums
- Connect with other scientists in your field

**Progress tracking**: Portal automatically monitors your Discord member count through the verification bot.

### 🌱 **Level 3: Community Growth & Engagement**

**Goal**: Build an active, engaged scientific community

**Requirements**:

- Grow to **10+ members**
- Share **25+ scientific papers** in your Discord
- Generate **100+ quality messages** of discussion

**Growth strategies**:

#### Building Membership

- **Academic networks**: Invite professors, grad students, postdocs
- **Conference connections**: Share with people you meet at conferences
- **Social media**: Post about your BioDAO on Twitter/LinkedIn
- **Research forums**: Share in Reddit communities like r/AskScience
- **Collaboration**: Partner with other research groups

#### Encouraging Paper Sharing

- **Lead by example**: Share 2-3 papers per week yourself
- **Create themed weeks**: "Breakthrough Fridays", "Method Mondays"
- **Discussion prompts**: Ask specific questions about shared papers
- **Paper summaries**: Encourage brief explanations of why papers matter

#### Fostering Quality Discussion

- **Ask open questions**: "What are the implications of this finding?"
- **Share personal insights**: Connect papers to your own research
- **Weekly topics**: Set discussion themes to guide conversation
- **Encourage participation**: Thank members for contributions

**[Screenshot placeholder: Discord community with papers and discussions]**

**Progress tracking**: Portal's bot automatically tracks:

- Member count updates in real-time
- Papers shared (detects academic links and PDFs)
- Message quality and quantity
- Your progress toward Level 3 goals

## 🔧 **Troubleshooting**

### Authentication Issues

**Problem**: "I can't log in" or "Verification email not arriving"
**Solutions**:

1. Check spam/junk folder
2. Wait 2-3 minutes (emails can be slow)
3. Try a different browser
4. Clear browser cache and cookies
5. Contact support via CoreAgent

### Discord Bot Issues

**Problem**: "The bot isn't tracking my Discord stats"
**Solutions**:

1. Ensure the bot has proper permissions in your server
2. Check that the bot is online (green status)
3. Re-share your Discord invite link with CoreAgent
4. Ask CoreAgent to refresh your server connection

### NFT Minting Issues

**Problem**: "NFT minting failed" or "Transaction didn't go through"
**Solutions**:

1. Refresh the page and try again
2. Ensure your wallet is properly connected
3. Check your internet connection
4. Wait a few minutes and retry (network congestion)

### Progress Tracking Issues

**Problem**: "My progress isn't updating"
**Solutions**:

1. Ask CoreAgent for a manual progress check
2. Ensure shared papers are in the correct Discord channels
3. Verify your Discord bot has reading permissions
4. Wait up to 1 hour for automatic updates
