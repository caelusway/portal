# Team Collaboration Guide

## 👥 **Building Your BioDAO Team**

Portal supports multi-member projects, allowing you to collaborate with co-founders, researchers, and team members throughout your BioDAO journey.

## 🚀 **Getting Started with Teams**

### When to Add Team Members

- **Before Level 2**: Add co-founders to help with Discord setup
- **During Level 3**: Recruit community managers and active researchers
- **Level 4+**: Bring in specialists for social media, partnerships, etc.

### Team Roles Available

- **Founder**: Full access to all project features and settings
- **Admin**: Manage team members, Discord, and most project aspects
- **Member**: Contribute to community growth and content creation

---

## 📧 **Inviting Team Members**

### Step 1: Access Team Settings

1. Navigate to **Settings** in Portal
2. Click the **"Team Management"** tab
3. You'll see your current team members and pending invitations

### Step 2: Send Invitations

1. Click **"Invite Team Member"**
2. Enter the person's email address
3. Select their role (Admin or Member)
4. Add a personal message (optional)
5. Click **"Send Invitation"**

**[Screenshot placeholder: Team invitation interface]**

### Step 3: Invitation Process

- **Email sent**: Invitee receives secure invitation link via email
- **7-day expiration**: Invitations expire automatically for security
- **Accept process**: Invitee creates Portal account and gains project access
- **Notification**: You're notified when invitations are accepted

---

## ⚙️ **Managing Your Team**

### Viewing Team Members

The team management interface shows:

- **Member name and email**
- **Current role** (Founder, Admin, Member)
- **Join date**
- **Last activity**

### Changing Roles

1. Find the team member in your team list
2. Click the **role dropdown** next to their name
3. Select new role (Admin ↔ Member)
4. Confirm the change
5. Member is notified of role update

**Note**: Only Founders can change roles and remove members.

### Removing Team Members

1. Click the **"Remove"** button next to a member's name
2. Confirm the removal in the popup dialog
3. Member immediately loses access to the project
4. Removed member is notified via email

## 🔐 **Role Permissions**

### 👑 **Founder Permissions**

- ✅ Full access to all project features
- ✅ Manage team members (invite, remove, change roles)
- ✅ Edit project settings and description
- ✅ Connect social media accounts
- ✅ View all Discord statistics and progress
- ✅ Mint NFTs and manage blockchain assets
- ✅ Access financial/funding opportunities

### 🛠️ **Admin Permissions**

- ✅ Invite new team members (as Members only)
- ✅ Edit project description and some settings
- ✅ Connect Discord and manage community
- ✅ View Discord statistics and progress
- ✅ Assist with NFT minting
- ✅ Manage social media connections
- ❌ Cannot remove team members or change roles
- ❌ Cannot delete the project

### 👤 **Member Permissions**

- ✅ View project progress and Discord statistics
- ✅ Contribute to Discord community management
- ✅ Participate in social media activities
- ✅ Access community growth tools
- ❌ Cannot invite new team members
- ❌ Cannot edit project settings
- ❌ Cannot connect social media accounts

## 🤝 **Collaboration Best Practices**

### Communication

- **Weekly check-ins**: Schedule regular team meetings
- **Discord coordination**: Use your project Discord for team chat
- **Role clarity**: Ensure everyone understands their responsibilities
- **Progress sharing**: Regularly update team on level progression

### Task Distribution

**For Level 2 (Discord Setup)**:

- **Founder**: Create Discord server, connect to Portal
- **Admin**: Set up channels, invite initial members
- **Members**: Invite personal networks, create welcoming content

**For Level 3 (Community Growth)**:

- **Founder**: Strategy and partnership outreach
- **Admin**: Daily Discord management, paper sharing
- **Members**: Content creation, member engagement

**For Level 4 (Social Presence)**:

- **Founder**: Main social media accounts, partnerships
- **Admin**: Content calendar, community cross-promotion
- **Members**: Individual social sharing, amplification

### Avoiding Conflicts

- **Clear expectations**: Define responsibilities upfront
- **Decision hierarchy**: Founder has final say on major decisions
- **Regular feedback**: Address issues early through open communication
- **Documentation**: Keep notes on decisions and responsibilities

## 🔄 **Invitation Management**

### Pending Invitations

Track sent invitations:

- **Status**: Pending, Accepted, Expired
- **Sent date** and **expiration date**
- **Invitee email** and **intended role**
- **Resend option** for non-expired invitations

### Resending Invitations

If someone didn't receive their invitation:

1. Find the pending invitation in your team settings
2. Click **"Resend Invitation"**
3. New email is sent with fresh 7-day expiration
4. Previous invitation link becomes invalid

### Revoking Invitations

To cancel a pending invitation:

1. Find the invitation in your pending list
2. Click **"Revoke Invitation"**
3. Invitation link becomes invalid immediately
4. You can send a new invitation if needed

## 🚨 **Team Security**

### Best Practices

- **Verify identity**: Confirm email addresses before inviting
- **Regular audits**: Review team member list monthly
- **Role principle**: Give minimum necessary permissions
- **Remove promptly**: Remove access immediately when someone leaves

### If Someone Leaves Your Project

1. **Remove from Portal**: Use team management to remove access
2. **Update Discord**: Remove from Discord admin roles if applicable
3. **Social media**: Disconnect their social accounts if linked
4. **Documentation**: Update team responsibilities and contacts

### If Your Account is Compromised

1. **Change passwords**: Update all linked social media accounts
2. **Review team**: Check for unauthorized team member additions
3. **Audit permissions**: Verify no unauthorized changes were made
4. **Contact support**: Report security concerns to Portal team

## 📞 **Team Support**

### Getting Help

- **CoreAgent assistance**: Ask about team features in Portal chat
- **Documentation**: Refer team members to this guide
- **Community support**: Connect with other teams in BioDAO Discord

### Common Questions

**Q: Can we have multiple Founders?**
A: Currently, only one Founder role per project. Consider promoting key co-founders to Admin.

**Q: What happens if the Founder leaves?**
A: Contact Portal support to transfer Founder status to another team member.

**Q: Can team members join from different institutions?**
A: Yes! Portal supports global collaboration across any organizations.

**Q: Is there a limit to team size?**
A: No limit on team members. Scale your team based on your project needs.
